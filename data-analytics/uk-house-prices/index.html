Checks sheet. Each test compares the calculations with the raw data. Separately, tools/check_workbook.py recalculates the workbook in LibreOffice and checks every real price, fall and annual figure against pandas.

A data quality catch. The World Bank CPI file's metadata says it holds a price index with 2005 = 100. It holds annual inflation rates (UK 1975: 24.2). The workbook treats them as rates and chains its own index; read as an index, real prices would be nonsense.

Data: Nationwide Building Society, UK house prices since 1952 (quarterly average price), via datasets/house-prices-uk. World Bank consumer price inflation, United Kingdom, via datasets/cpi. Both ODC PDDL 1.0. Nationwide's method changed over time, so long-run comparisons are approximate. No earnings data, so this is not an affordability ratio.
