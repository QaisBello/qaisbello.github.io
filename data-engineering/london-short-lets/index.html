05 · How sure?

The count depends on one assumption

Occupancy is modelled from reviews, so the answer depends on what share of stays leave one. Even at the most cautious published rate, thousands of homes stay above 90 nights.

06 · The metric layer

How every number on this page is made

Raw snapshot91,954 listings, 33.6 million calendar nights and 2.3 million reviews from Inside Airbnb.
Staged in SQLDuckDB models type the data, rebuild 12-month reviews from the raw log and window each calendar to 365 nights.
CheckedQuality checks run before anything is published. An error stops the build.
Defined onceEvery metric lives in one YAML file with a version and a hash. The page, Power BI and Excel read the same output.
Quality checks and definition history
Data: Inside Airbnb, London, scraped 18-22 September 2026. Occupancy is Inside Airbnb's review-based model, reproduced exactly from the raw reviews: a 50% review rate, a 4.6-night average stay (or the minimum stay if longer), capped at 70% of the year. Modelled nights cover the 12 months before the scrape; the 90-night limit applies per calendar year. Listing locations are approximate (Airbnb moves them up to 150 m). Not legal advice: a home above 90 nights may have planning permission. Analysis by Qais Adeniji-Bello.
