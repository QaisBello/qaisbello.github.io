Late parcels,
angry customers

100,000 real orders from Olist, a Brazilian online marketplace, modelled into a tested data warehouse with dbt. The question the warehouse answers: what makes a customer leave a bad review? Mostly, a parcel that arrives after the date it was promised.

The stack
dbt CoreSQLDuckDBPostgreSQLPython (load)GitHub Actions
Raw CSVs are loaded as text, then dbt types, cleans, tests and models them into a star schema: 4 dimensions, 2 facts and 4 marts. The same models build on DuckDB and PostgreSQL, and every push re-runs all of them with their tests.

Late by a few days, rated one star

Average review (1 to 5) for delivered orders, by how the delivery compared with the date promised at checkout.

Where parcels run late

Share of delivered orders that arrived after the promised date, by the customer's state (states with 300+ orders). Orange: above the national rate. Most sellers are in São Paulo (SP), so distance from SP drives lateness.

Orders by month

Orders placed (canceled and unavailable excluded), with the share delivered late. The November 2017 peak is Black Friday; the late-delivery spike that follows hit in February and March 2018.
